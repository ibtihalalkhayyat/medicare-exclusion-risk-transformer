# Public data access and release policy

## Public

- Research objectives, bounded aggregate governance results, temporal-protocol descriptions.
- Synthetic example input format: **24 fictional profiles**, **140 input fields** each.
- **504 precomputed outputs** for synthetic examples from the original frozen model and calibration.
- Safe source-code excerpts, checksums and technical validation scripts after review.
- Static browser interface and links to the public Hugging Face synthetic dataset/Space.

## Restricted or unpublished

- Medicare CMS provider-level raw or derived real data, NPIs and claims/service-level extracts.
- HHS–OIG LEIE source registry rows, linkage files, provider-level event labels and individual risk scores.
- Frozen Transformer checkpoint, fitted preprocessing, category vocabulary, Platt coefficients, and other private assets.
- Complete internal Colab notebooks and Google Drive path maps, secrets, access tokens.
- Sealed 2023 final-test outcomes or any data derived from opening them.

## Public safety and scientific interpretation

The research outcome is an **observed administrative exclusion**, not confirmed fraud. Public synthetic profiles do not support clinical, insurance, sanction, payment, or individual provider decisions. Public release of additional code, weights, data, or derivatives requires a separate provenance, licensing, privacy and re-identification review.

**The public synthetic dataset may have its own CC0 license; this does not authorize redistribution of real CMS/LEIE sources, model assets, or other private files.**
